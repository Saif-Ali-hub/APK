HOW TO PLAY

🏹
Tap Free Arrows: Arrows fly forward in the direction their head points. Only tap arrows that have an open escape path!
💥
Avoid Collisions: If an arrow hits another arrow, it recoils and you lose 1 life (❤️). You have 3 lives!
🔍
Zoom & Pan: On big levels, pinch or use the zoom buttons (+ / −) to inspect tricky overlapping sections.
🧭
Grid & Route Guides: Toggle the 🧭 GRID button anytime to preview the exact escape paths of every arrow.
